LEGAL

Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 3, 2026

1. Who operates Klippy Studio

Klippy Studio is an independently operated desktop tool maintained by the GitHub user kharo23 ("Klippy Studio", "we", "us"). This policy explains how information is handled when a user connects a TikTok account and uploads a selected video draft.

2. Information processed

Depending on the permissions granted by the user, Klippy Studio may process:

  • TikTok OAuth credentials, including access and refresh tokens;
  • basic TikTok account identifiers and profile information made available through the authorized scope;
  • video files and technical metadata explicitly selected by the user for upload;
  • upload identifiers and status information returned by TikTok.

Klippy Studio does not request access to private messages, contacts, passwords, or unrelated TikTok content.

3. How information is used

Information is used only to authenticate the user, send a user-selected video to that user's TikTok inbox as a draft, display upload status, refresh authorization when necessary, and diagnose errors initiated by the user.

4. Local storage and retention

The current desktop version stores authorization credentials and workflow state locally on the user's device. Klippy Studio does not operate a separate remote user database. Credentials remain available until they expire, the user disconnects the account, removes local application data, or revokes access in TikTok. Selected video files remain in the locations controlled by the user.

5. Sharing and third-party services

Selected videos and the data required to perform an upload are transmitted to TikTok only after an explicit user action. TikTok processes that information under its own terms and privacy policy. This website is hosted by GitHub Pages, which may process standard network and access-log information under the GitHub Privacy Statement. We do not sell personal information or share it for behavioral advertising.

6. Legal basis

Where applicable, processing is based on the user's consent and on the steps requested by the user to provide the upload functionality. The user may withdraw authorization at any time by disconnecting Klippy Studio from TikTok.

7. Security

Klippy Studio is designed to keep client secrets out of public files and to store user tokens locally. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, and users remain responsible for securing their device and GitHub or TikTok accounts.

8. User choices and rights

Users may revoke TikTok authorization, remove locally stored credentials, or request information about the handling described in this policy. Depending on location, users may also have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, or object to the processing of personal information.

9. Children

Klippy Studio is not directed to children and is intended only for users permitted to operate a TikTok account and authorize developer applications under applicable terms and law.

10. Changes

We may update this policy when the application's functionality or legal requirements change. The date at the top identifies the latest version.

11. Contact

Privacy questions may be submitted through the public maintainer profile at github.com/kharo23. Do not include access tokens, passwords, or sensitive personal information in a public GitHub message or issue.
