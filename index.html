KLIPPY STUDIO

Creator-controlled video drafts.

A desktop workflow for reviewing authorized short-form clips and sending selected videos to the creator's TikTok inbox as drafts. Nothing is ever published automatically — the creator opens TikTok, reviews the draft, and decides.

How it works

  1. Import. The creator adds a long-form video they own or are licensed to use — a podcast episode, an interview, a recorded stream.
  2. Prepare. Klippy Studio transcribes the audio, cuts the selected moments, reframes them to a 9:16 vertical format and burns in subtitles.
  3. Review. Every clip is shown in a contact sheet with its caption and hashtags. The creator keeps or discards each one.
  4. Transfer. Approved clips are sent to the creator's own TikTok inbox as drafts, ready to be finished and published from the TikTok app.

How Klippy Studio uses TikTok

Klippy Studio integrates with two TikTok for Developers products. Each is used for a single, specific purpose:

Login Kit — user.info.basic
Authenticates the creator with OAuth (state and PKCE) and displays the connected profile name, so it is always clear which account will receive the content.
Content Posting API — video.upload
Transfers a creator-selected video file to that same account's TikTok inbox as a draft. The creator then edits the caption, chooses the settings and publishes from within the TikTok app.

Direct Post is not enabled. Klippy Studio does not request the video.publish scope and has no ability to publish to a profile. Every post is a human decision made inside TikTok.

What Klippy Studio does not do

  • It does not publish, schedule or post content automatically.
  • It does not read, collect or analyse other users' TikTok data.
  • It does not manage accounts on behalf of third parties.
  • It does not automate browsing, following, liking or commenting.
  • It does not buy, sell or artificially inflate engagement.

Your data

Klippy Studio runs entirely on the creator's own computer. There is no server, no account to create and no cloud storage. Access and refresh tokens are kept in local files on that machine and are used only to talk to TikTok's own API.

No data is sold, rented or shared with third parties, and no analytics or advertising trackers are used. Access can be revoked at any time from TikTok's settings, and deleting the application folder removes everything stored locally.

Requirements

  • A desktop computer running macOS.
  • A TikTok account owned by the person using the application.
  • Source video the creator owns or is licensed to use. Klippy Studio does not provide, host or distribute any content.

Questions

Will my clips be posted without me seeing them?
No. Clips arrive as drafts in your TikTok inbox. Nothing appears on your profile until you publish it yourself from the TikTok app.
Can it access my followers or messages?
No. The only profile information requested is your basic public profile, used to show which account is connected.
How do I disconnect it?
Revoke the application from your TikTok account settings. It will immediately lose the ability to send anything.
